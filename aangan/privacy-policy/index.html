1. Who we are

Aangan ("the app") is a mobile application developed and operated by Kanishk Sharma ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. The same promises are shown inside the app, in Hindi and English, on the Parivaar tab.

2. What we collect: nothing

Aangan does not collect, receive, or transmit any user data. The app requires no account and makes no network requests — nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

The information the app works with — your chosen deity, your ritual and puja history, your city, and the panchang computed from it — is created and stored entirely on your phone. We never see it, and no copy exists anywhere else.

The app may keep a small technical log on your device to help it run correctly. Like everything else, this log is never uploaded or shared — it stays on your phone and is removed when you erase the app's data.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data — there is nothing to share. The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

4. Children's privacy

Aangan is made for the whole family, including children. Because the app collects no data at all — from anyone — no personal information about any child is ever collected, stored by us, or shared. The app contains no advertising and no third-party content.

5. Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone, and you are in full control of it:

  • In the app: Settings → delete everything erases all app data at once.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Since we keep no copy on any server, deleting the data from your phone deletes it everywhere — there is nothing left for us to erase, and nothing we could recover.

6. Data security

Your information never travels over the internet, which removes the most common privacy risks entirely. Protecting the data on your phone works the same way as for any content on your device: your phone's own lock screen and security settings protect it.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Aangan ever changes how data is handled — for example, if an optional, consent-based feature that transmits data is added — this policy will be updated before that change ships, with a new effective date, and the app's store listing will be updated to match.
